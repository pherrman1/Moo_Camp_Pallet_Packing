\begin{abstract}
This document states the mixed-integer linear model constructed by
\texttt{gurobi\_coordinate\_solver.py}.  Unlike the position-indexed model in
\texttt{MILP\_formulation.tex}, it does not enumerate every possible placement.
Each physical box receives an integer coordinate, a pallet assignment, and an
orientation.  Pairwise direction variables enforce three-dimensional
non-overlap.  Optional support constraints use the exact horizontal
intersection area of two boxes.  The implemented objective is solely to
minimize the number of used pallets.  The document first gives the default
\texttt{legacy} formulation and then records the changes made by the selectable
\texttt{reduced\_exact} formulation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and source of the model}

The solver reads an MCPP/PL-100 JSON instance and a JSON configuration file.
The instance supplies the pallet and physical boxes.  The configuration
supplies the spatial grid, maximum candidate-pallet count, rotation and support
modes, symmetry options, and Gurobi stopping parameters.  All spatial
quantities in the optimization model are integer numbers of grid cells.

The implementation currently uses box geometry and identity information, but
does \emph{not} impose weight, food--chemical, fragility, or retrieval-priority
constraints.  Those attributes are parsed, used in the key that recognizes
identical boxes, and reproduced in solution files.  This distinction is
important: their presence in the input does not mean they are active physical
constraints in this coordinate model.

\section{Input processing and primitive data}

\subsection{Grid conversion}

Let $g\in\Z_{>0}$ be \texttt{grid\_mm}.  It comes from the configuration,
unless its value is the string \texttt{input}, in which case it comes from
\texttt{meta.grid\_mm} in the instance (default 50 mm if absent).

Let $(L^{\rm mm},W^{\rm mm},H^{\rm mm})$ be the pallet dimensions read from
one of the accepted JSON schemas.  The model dimensions are conservatively
floored:
\begin{equation}
 (L,W,H)=\left(
 \left\lfloor\frac{L^{\rm mm}}g\right\rfloor,
 \left\lfloor\frac{W^{\rm mm}}g\right\rfloor,
 \left\lfloor\frac{H^{\rm mm}}g\right\rfloor\right).
 \label{eq:pallet-rounding}
\end{equation}
For box $i$, let $(\ell_i^{\rm mm},w_i^{\rm mm},h_i^{\rm mm})$ be its
dimensions after schema normalization.  Its unrotated grid dimensions are
rounded upward:
\begin{equation}
 (\ell_i,w_i,h_i)=\left(
 \max\left\{1,\left\lceil\frac{\ell_i^{\rm mm}}g\right\rceil\right\},
 \max\left\{1,\left\lceil\frac{w_i^{\rm mm}}g\right\rceil\right\},
 \max\left\{1,\left\lceil\frac{h_i^{\rm mm}}g\right\rceil\right\}\right).
 \label{eq:item-rounding}
\end{equation}
Flooring the container and ceiling the boxes makes the discretization
conservative: a grid-feasible packing does not exceed the physical pallet
boundary, apart from ordinary floating-point input tolerances used in parsing.

\subsection{Sets and indices}

\begin{align}
 \Items&:=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}, && i,j\in\Items,\\
 \Pallets&:=\{0,\ldots,P^{\max}-1\}, && p\in\Pallets,\\
 \Pairs&:=\{(i,j)\in\Items^2:i<j\},\\
 \OPairs&:=\{(i,j)\in\Items^2:i\ne j\}.
\end{align}
Here $n$ is the number of records in the instance's \texttt{items} or
\texttt{boxes} array.  $P^{\max}$ is \texttt{max\_pallets}; for batch solves
it can be raised to a benchmark-provided pallet upper bound.  Candidate
pallets are identical and their indices have no physical meaning.

For each $i$, preprocessing constructs a list of distinct permitted oriented
dimension triples
\begin{equation}
 \Orients_i=\{0,\ldots,m_i-1\},\qquad
 (d^x_{io},d^y_{io},d^z_{io})\in\Z_{>0}^3.
\end{equation}
The list comes from \texttt{rotation\_mode}:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.8cm,style=nextline]
 \item[\texttt{none}] only $(\ell_i,w_i,h_i)$;
 \item[\texttt{yaw}] the original orientation and a swap of the horizontal
 axes;
 \item[\texttt{metadata}] the same yaw-only rule (and thus no extra behavior
 beyond \texttt{yaw} in the current code);
 \item[\texttt{six}] all distinct axis permutations, except that a box with
 \texttt{upright\_only=true} is still restricted to yaw rotations.
\end{description}
Repeated triples caused by equal side lengths are removed while preserving
their first occurrence.

The instance also supplies box ID, SKU, weight, volume in dm$^3$, family,
food flag, chemical flag, fragile flag, upright-only flag, and retrieval
priority.  ID and the original millimetre dimensions are reporting fields.
SKU, grid dimensions, weight, family, the three handling flags, upright-only,
and priority form the identical-item grouping key.  Of these, only dimensions
and upright-only status otherwise affect the current optimization model.

\section{Default (\texttt{legacy}) decision variables}

The legacy formulation stores a separate local geometric state for every
box--pallet combination.  Variables belonging to an unassigned combination
are forced to zero by the boundary equations.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{2.3cm}p{2.8cm}p{2.5cm}p{6.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
Variable & Index & Domain & Meaning \\
\midrule
\endhead
$u_p$ & $p\in\Pallets$ & $\B$ & 1 iff candidate pallet $p$ is used.\\
$a_{ip}$ & $i\in\Items,p\in\Pallets$ & $\B$ & 1 iff box $i$ is assigned to pallet $p$.\\
$r_{iop}$ & $i,p,o\in\Orients_i$ & $\B$ & 1 iff $i$ is on $p$ with orientation $o$.\\
$x_{ip},y_{ip},z_{ip}$ & $i,p$ & integer, respectively $[0,L],[0,W],[0,H]$ & lower-left-bottom coordinates.\\
$\bar x_{ip},\bar y_{ip},\bar z_{ip}$ & $i,p$ & integer, same bounds & right, front, and top coordinates.\\
$b_{ip}$ & $i,p$ & $\Z_{\ge0}$ & oriented base area; zero if unassigned.\\
$\lambda^x_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\OPairs,p$ & $\B$ & 1 exactly when $i$ ends at or before $j$ starts on $x$.\\
$\lambda^y_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\OPairs,p$ & $\B$ & analogous ``in front of'' relation on $y$.\\
$\lambda^z_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\OPairs,p$ & $\B$ & analogous ``below'' relation on $z$.\\
$o^x_{ijp},o^y_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\Pairs,p$ & $\B$ & 1 iff assigned boxes overlap strictly on the named horizontal axis.\\
$o^{xy}_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\Pairs,p$ & $\B$ & logical AND $o^x_{ijp}\land o^y_{ijp}$.\\
$f_{jp}$ & $j,p$ & $\B$ & 1 iff assigned box $j$ is on the floor.\\
$c_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\OPairs,p$ & $\B$ & 1 may certify that $i$ directly supports $j$.\\
$q^x_{ijp},q^y_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\Pairs,p$ & integer & exact horizontal overlap lengths when area support is active.\\
$A_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\Pairs,p$ & continuous or integer & exact footprint-intersection area.\\
$S_{ijp}$ & $(i,j)\in\OPairs,p$ & continuous or integer & $A_{\min\{i,j\},\max\{i,j\},p}$ when $c_{ijp}=1$, and zero otherwise.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The area auxiliaries are continuous by default in the legacy model, although
their defining equations give integral values at integer coordinates.  Setting
\texttt{area\_auxiliary\_type} to \texttt{integer} declares them integral.

Define the following convenient linear expressions:
\begin{align}
 D^x_{ip}&:=\sum_{o\in\Orients_i}d^x_{io}r_{iop},&
 D^y_{ip}&:=\sum_{o\in\Orients_i}d^y_{io}r_{iop},&
 D^z_{ip}&:=\sum_{o\in\Orients_i}d^z_{io}r_{iop}.
\end{align}

\section{Objective}

The single implemented optimization criterion is
\begin{equation}
 \min\quad \sum_{p\in\Pallets}u_p.
 \label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
Weight, center of mass, accessibility, family separation, and similar project
attributes do not appear in this objective.

\section{Assignment, orientation, and containment}

Each physical box is assigned once, and it has one orientation precisely on
its assigned pallet:
\begin{align}
 \sum_{p\in\Pallets}a_{ip}&=1 &&\forall i\in\Items,\label{eq:assign-once}\\
 \sum_{o\in\Orients_i}r_{iop}&=a_{ip}
 &&\forall i\in\Items,\ p\in\Pallets.\label{eq:one-orientation}
\end{align}
Assignment activates a pallet, and activation is exact:
\begin{align}
 a_{ip}&\le u_p &&\forall i,p,\label{eq:activate}\\
 u_p&\le\sum_{i\in\Items}a_{ip} &&\forall p.\label{eq:used-has-box}
\end{align}

The endpoint variables are defined by
\begin{align}
 \bar x_{ip}&=x_{ip}+D^x_{ip},&
 \bar y_{ip}&=y_{ip}+D^y_{ip},&
 \bar z_{ip}&=z_{ip}+D^z_{ip},\label{eq:endpoints}\\
 b_{ip}&=\sum_{o\in\Orients_i}d^x_{io}d^y_{io}r_{iop}.
 \label{eq:base-area}
\end{align}
Containment and the zeroing of inactive local states follow from
\begin{equation}
 \bar x_{ip}\le La_{ip},\qquad
 \bar y_{ip}\le Wa_{ip},\qquad
 \bar z_{ip}\le Ha_{ip}
 \qquad\forall i,p.
 \label{eq:containment}
\end{equation}

The volume inequalities are valid but not sufficient packing conditions:
\begin{align}
 \sum_{i\in\Items}v_i a_{ip}&\le Vu_p &&\forall p,\label{eq:volume-capacity}\\
 \sum_{p\in\Pallets}u_p&\ge K^{\rm vol}.\label{eq:volume-lb}
\end{align}
In addition, the implementation fixes $u_p=1$ for
$p=0,\ldots,K^{\rm vol}-1$.  This is valid because pallet labels are
interchangeable and used pallets are ordered as a prefix.

\section{Pairwise non-overlap}

For every ordered pair and pallet, forward big-$M$ inequalities give the
meaning of the direction variables:
\begin{align}
 \bar x_{ip}&\le x_{jp}+L(1-\lambda^x_{ijp}),\label{eq:left-forward}\\
 \bar y_{ip}&\le y_{jp}+W(1-\lambda^y_{ijp}),\\
 \bar z_{ip}&\le z_{jp}+H(1-\lambda^z_{ijp}).
\end{align}
They can be 1 only for two boxes assigned to that pallet:
\begin{equation}
 \lambda^a_{ijp}\le a_{ip},\qquad \lambda^a_{ijp}\le a_{jp}
 \quad\forall(i,j)\in\OPairs,p,\ a\in\{x,y,z\}.
 \label{eq:direction-activation}
\end{equation}
The reverse inequalities make each binary an exact strict-separation
indicator at integer coordinates.  For example,
\begin{equation}
 x_{jp}\le \bar x_{ip}-1
 +L\bigl(\lambda^x_{ijp}+2-a_{ip}-a_{jp}\bigr),
 \label{eq:left-reverse}
\end{equation}
with analogous inequalities on $y$ and $z$.  If both boxes are assigned and
$\lambda^x_{ijp}=0$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:left-reverse} says
$x_{jp}\le\bar x_{ip}-1$, i.e., the two integer intervals overlap strictly or
$j$ lies to the left.  The one-cell term is the discrete analogue of a strict
inequality.

Two boxes assigned to the same pallet must be separated on at least one axis:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 &\lambda^x_{ijp}+\lambda^x_{jip}
 +\lambda^y_{ijp}+\lambda^y_{jip}
 +\lambda^z_{ijp}+\lambda^z_{jip}\\
 &\hspace{3cm}\ge a_{ip}+a_{jp}-1
 \qquad\forall(i,j)\in\Pairs,\ p\in\Pallets.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:nonoverlap}
\end{equation}

The horizontal overlap indicators used by support are defined by the standard
binary AND logic
\begin{align}
 o^x_{ijp}&\le a_{ip},a_{jp},1-\lambda^x_{ijp},1-\lambda^x_{jip},\nonumber\\
 o^x_{ijp}&\ge a_{ip}+a_{jp}-1-\lambda^x_{ijp}-\lambda^x_{jip},\label{eq:overlap-x}\\
 o^y_{ijp}&\le a_{ip},a_{jp},1-\lambda^y_{ijp},1-\lambda^y_{jip},\nonumber\\
 o^y_{ijp}&\ge a_{ip}+a_{jp}-1-\lambda^y_{ijp}-\lambda^y_{jip},\label{eq:overlap-y}\\
 o^{xy}_{ijp}&\le o^x_{ijp},\quad o^{xy}_{ijp}\le o^y_{ijp},\quad
 o^{xy}_{ijp}\ge o^x_{ijp}+o^y_{ijp}-1.
 \label{eq:overlap-xy}
\end{align}
Comma-separated upper bounds in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:overlap-x}--\eqref{eq:overlap-y}
denote one inequality for each listed right-hand side.

\section{Support modes}

Support is selected by \texttt{support.mode}.  In \texttt{off} mode, none of
the variables or constraints in this section are created.  Otherwise the floor
indicator is defined by
\begin{align}
 f_{jp}&\le a_{jp},& z_{jp}&\le H(1-f_{jp}),&
 z_{jp}&\ge a_{jp}-f_{jp}.
 \label{eq:floor}
\end{align}
Thus, for an assigned box, $f_{jp}=1$ exactly at $z_{jp}=0$.

A possible direct-support certificate satisfies
\begin{align}
 c_{ijp}&\le a_{ip},a_{jp},\lambda^z_{ijp},
 o^{xy}_{\min\{i,j\},\max\{i,j\},p},\label{eq:contact-bounds}\\
 z_{jp}-\bar z_{ip}&\le H(1-c_{ijp}),\qquad
 \bar z_{ip}-z_{jp}\le H(1-c_{ijp}).
 \label{eq:contact-height}
\end{align}
Consequently, $c_{ijp}=1$ implies same-pallet assignment, positive footprint
overlap, $i$ below $j$, and exact vertical contact $\bar z_{ip}=z_{jp}$.
The converse need not be forced, but the support requirement forces enough
valid certificates to cover every raised box.

\subsection{Direct support}

In \texttt{direct} mode, any positive horizontal overlap with at least one
directly touching box is sufficient:
\begin{equation}
 f_{jp}+\sum_{i\in\Items\setminus\{j\}}c_{ijp}\ge a_{jp}
 \qquad\forall j,p.
 \label{eq:direct-support}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Fractional and full support}

In \texttt{fraction} and \texttt{full} modes, the exact rectangle-intersection
lengths are
\begin{align}
 q^x_{ijp}&=\max\{0,\min(\bar x_{ip},\bar x_{jp})-
                         \max(x_{ip},x_{jp})\},\label{eq:qx}\\
 q^y_{ijp}&=\max\{0,\min(\bar y_{ip},\bar y_{jp})-
                         \max(y_{ip},y_{jp})\},\label{eq:qy}\\
 A_{ijp}&=q^x_{ijp}q^y_{ijp}.
 \label{eq:area-product}
\end{align}
The minima and maxima are inserted as Gurobi general constraints.  The product
in Eq.~\eqref{eq:area-product} is linearized exactly.  In the default
\texttt{compact} formulation, one overlap length is represented in binary,
$q^x=\sum_k2^k\beta_k$, each product $\beta_kq^y$ receives the usual three
linear inequalities, and $A=\sum_k2^k(\beta_kq^y)$.  Configuration alternatives
\texttt{lookup\_1d} and \texttt{lookup\_2d} enumerate feasible orientation and
relative-offset states but produce the same exact overlap area.

The area counted as support is the contact-gated value
\begin{align}
 0\le S_{ijp}&\le A_{\min\{i,j\},\max\{i,j\},p},\nonumber\\
 S_{ijp}&\le A^{\max}c_{ijp},\nonumber\\
 S_{ijp}&\ge A_{\min\{i,j\},\max\{i,j\},p}
                  -A^{\max}(1-c_{ijp}).
 \label{eq:support-area-gate}
\end{align}

Let $\widehat\alpha=1$ in \texttt{full} mode and let
$\widehat\alpha=\alpha=r/s$ in \texttt{fraction} mode.  The implemented row is
\begin{equation}
 s\sum_{i\ne j}S_{ijp}+r\bar A_j f_{jp}\ge r b_{jp}
 \qquad\forall j,p.
 \label{eq:fraction-support}
\end{equation}
For a raised assigned box, $f_{jp}=0$, and this is exactly the required
supported-area fraction.  For a floor box, the term $r\bar A_jf_{jp}$ makes the
row automatically feasible because $b_{jp}\le\bar A_j$.

\section{Symmetry-breaking constraints}

All candidate pallets are interchangeable.  The following always enforces a
prefix of used pallet labels:
\begin{equation}
 u_p\ge u_{p+1}\qquad p=0,\ldots,P^{\max}-2.
 \label{eq:pallet-prefix}
\end{equation}
Three additional families are controlled by configuration (all are enabled in
the supplied default file):
\begin{align}
 \sum_i v_i a_{ip}&\ge\sum_i v_i a_{i,p+1}
 &&\text{if \texttt{order\_pallet\_loads}},\label{eq:load-order}\\
 a_{0,0}&=1 &&\text{if \texttt{fix\_first\_item}}.\label{eq:first-item}
\end{align}

For \texttt{order\_identical\_items}, boxes are grouped by the exact tuple
\begin{equation}
 (\mathrm{SKU},(\ell_i,w_i,h_i),\mathrm{weight},\mathrm{family},
 \mathrm{food},\mathrm{chemical},\mathrm{fragile},
 \mathrm{uprightOnly},\mathrm{priority}).
\end{equation}
For consecutive indices $(i,j)$ in any group,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{p\in\Pallets}p\,a_{ip}\le\sum_{p\in\Pallets}p\,a_{jp}.
 \label{eq:identical-pallet}
\end{equation}
Define the injective integer encoding
\begin{equation}
 \phi(x,y,z):=x(W+1)(H+1)+y(H+1)+z.
\end{equation}
On any pallet to which both boxes are assigned, their local positions are
ordered:
\begin{equation}
 \phi(x_{ip},y_{ip},z_{ip})\le
 \phi(x_{jp},y_{jp},z_{jp})+M^{\rm loc}(2-a_{ip}-a_{jp}).
 \label{eq:identical-location}
\end{equation}
These constraints remove permutations of records treated as identical; they
do not add a physical packing rule.

\section{The \texttt{reduced\_exact} variant}

If \texttt{model\_variant} is \texttt{reduced\_exact}, identical candidate
pallets no longer receive copies of a box's geometry.  The assignment variables
$a_{ip}$ and pallet-use variables $u_p$ remain, but orientation, coordinates,
endpoints, direction relations, overlap lengths, areas, floor indicators, and
contact variables drop their pallet index.  Thus, for example,
\begin{equation}
 r_{io}\in\B,\quad (x_i,y_i,z_i)\in\Z_{\ge0}^3,\quad
 \lambda^a_{ij}\in\B,\quad c_{ij}\in\B.
\end{equation}
Only orientations that individually fit within $(L,W,H)$ are retained.  The
global containment equations are
\begin{align}
 \sum_o r_{io}&=1,\\
 \bar x_i=x_i+\sum_od^x_{io}r_{io}&\le L,quad
 \bar y_i=y_i+\sum_od^y_{io}r_{io}\le W,quad
 \bar z_i=z_i+\sum_od^z_{io}r_{io}\le H.
\end{align}
Non-overlap is activated only when a pair shares a candidate pallet:
\begin{equation}
 \lambda^x_{ij}+\lambda^x_{ji}+\lambda^y_{ij}+\lambda^y_{ji}
 +\lambda^z_{ij}+\lambda^z_{ji}
 \ge a_{ip}+a_{jp}-1
 \quad\forall(i,j)\in\Pairs,p.
\end{equation}
The global direction variables are exact because their forward and reverse
rows no longer need assignment deactivation terms.

For support, $f_j$ indicates $z_j=0$.  A contact $c_{ij}$ is permitted only if
$i$ and $j$ have the same assignment vector; this is enforced for every $p$ by
\begin{equation}
 c_{ij}\le1-a_{ip}+a_{jp},\qquad
 c_{ij}\le1+a_{ip}-a_{jp}.
\end{equation}
It also enforces $\bar z_i=z_j$.  In direct mode it is gated by positive
horizontal overlap.  In fractional/full mode its gated intersection area is
used exactly as above.  The support row becomes
\begin{equation}
 s\sum_{i\ne j}S_{ij}+r\bar A_jf_j
 \ge r\sum_{o\in\Orients_j}d^x_{jo}d^y_{jo}r_{jo}.
\end{equation}
The objective, volume bounds, assignment, activation, and symmetry logic are
otherwise the same.  This variant is exact for the same discretized problem:
because every pallet has identical dimensions, a box needs only one local
coordinate and orientation for the single pallet to which it is assigned.

\section{What is and is not guaranteed}

The selected model is exact with respect to integer grid coordinates, its
allowed orientation list, its configured support mode, and at most
$P^{\max}$ candidate pallets.  The conservative rounding may exclude a
continuous packing that relies on sub-grid dimensions or positions.  The
volume bound strengthens the model but never replaces geometric non-overlap.

The model does not currently enforce pallet payload weight, center of mass,
density, food--chemical separation, fragile-box restrictions, family
compatibility, or retrieval accessibility.  These fields remain available for
future extensions and for reporting, but no such constraint should be inferred
from the present solver output.
